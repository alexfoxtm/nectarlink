libyuv in CameraX core 1.6.2 (androidx.camera:camera-core:1.6.2)
Used by Waypair Link for camera capture.

Shipped binary: camera-core-1.6.2.aar from Google Maven, SHA-256
ebd66f090414f4c12f23a55b5023aeee1b096da8a06c02a0b6fb3520c4ddadf6. Its native
library lib/<abi>/libimage_processing_util_jni.so (arm64-v8a SHA-256
0292b063fafccf734472cbb59588e756c2dbca907c72021ba6fbf126385508a2) contains
libyuv, linked statically. The AAR carries only the Apache-2.0 text
(Apache-2.0.txt); its POM also declares BSD-3-Clause, for libyuv.

Source: CameraX 1.6.2 is commit 9046f07948c1e7a89aaec581463730f4d666611b of
https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/support (the end of the
1.6.2 range in the CameraX release notes). There:
  camera/camera-core/src/main/cpp/CMakeLists.txt links
    image_processing_util_jni with libyuv::yuv (find_package(libyuv)).
  camera/camera-core/build.gradle says "This library includes code from
    libyuv, which is licensed under the BSD-3-Clause license."
  external/libyuv/build.gradle builds the static "yuv" target from the
    checkout's external/libyuv
    (https://android.googlesource.com/platform/external/libyuv).

The exact libyuv revision in this build is not recorded by the AAR or by that
commit. The LICENSE below is the one at
https://android.googlesource.com/platform/external/libyuv main
(daeff19e274f52902036ae141c56e946018bc4e8, 2025-03-26), SHA-256
